A spreadsheet is enough for tracking SaaS renewals when you have a small book, one owner who updates it every week, and accounts that each get regular personal attention. It stops being enough when renewals outgrow what one person can review, because a spreadsheet only knows what someone types in: it cannot read emails, tickets or usage, and it cannot do the follow-up. That is where renewal software like Renewal360 earns its place.

We make renewal software, so read this with that in mind. We have tried to be fair: plenty of teams should keep their spreadsheet for now.

What spreadsheets do well

  • Free and familiar. Everyone knows how to use one.
  • Flexible. Add any column you like, any time.
  • Good for a small book. With a few dozen accounts and an owner who knows each customer well, a spreadsheet plus a calendar works.
  • Easy to share. Leaders can look at the same file.

Where spreadsheets break down

  • They only know what is typed in. The champion's silence, the open escalation and the usage drop never reach the sheet unless someone notices and records them.
  • They go stale. Busy weeks mean missed updates, and nobody can tell which rows are current.
  • They do not act. Every check-in, reminder and who-signs email still has to be written by hand.
  • They hide risk in averages. A status column of "green" says nothing about why.
  • They depend on one person. When the owner is away, the process stops.

When a spreadsheet is the right answer

  • You have a small number of renewals a quarter and each one gets personal attention anyway.
  • One person owns renewals and updates the sheet every week without fail.
  • Churn is rare and never a surprise.

If that is you, keep the spreadsheet and revisit when the book grows.

How to work out whether switching pays

Add up two things: the hours your team spends each week keeping the sheet current and writing renewal emails, and the revenue you lost last year from accounts that showed warning signs months ahead. If the hours, plus even a fraction of that lost revenue, exceed the cost of a tool, switching pays. The full method is in the hidden cost of a manual renewal process.
