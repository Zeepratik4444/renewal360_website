When is Gainsight the right choice?

Gainsight is one of the most complete customer success platforms available. It is the right choice when:

  • You have a large CS organisation, often with several teams and segments, and a CS operations function to configure and maintain the platform.
  • You need deep, configurable health scorecards, success plans and digital journeys across the whole customer lifecycle.
  • You want CS, customer education, community or in-product engagement from one vendor.
  • You have the time and budget for a structured implementation.

The trade-off is effort. Gainsight rewards teams that invest in setting it up and running it. Smaller teams often find they pay for, and maintain, much more than they use. We cover options for those teams in Gainsight alternatives for growing SaaS teams.

When is Totango the right choice?

Totango is built around modular, goal-based building blocks for onboarding, adoption, renewal and expansion programmes. It is a good fit when:

  • You want to roll out customer programmes step by step, using ready-made templates.
  • Your team is organised around lifecycle programmes as much as around individual accounts.
  • You are a mid-market or enterprise team that wants structure without building everything from scratch.

Since the 2024 combination with Catalyst, Totango's product line has been coming together, so it is worth asking the vendor which product and plan fits your team today.

When is Renewal360 the right choice?

Renewal360 does one job: it makes sure no renewal is lost for want of attention. It is the right first move when:

  • Renewals are the urgent problem. You keep learning about churn too late, and the follow-up depends on memory or a spreadsheet.
  • Your team is small or growing. You do not have, or want, a dedicated admin to run a CS platform.
  • You want the work done, not just flagged. Renewal360 checks each renewal for what is missing, such as a confirmed champion, a known signer or an agreed price, and drafts the next step for approval.
  • You want to start this week. Import a spreadsheet and see your renewals the same day; connect your CRM and inbox when you are ready.

Renewal360 is not trying to be a full lifecycle suite. If you need community, customer education or deep in-product engagement, a broader platform will fit better.

What does Renewal360 do that a broad platform might not?

  • Readiness, not just a score. Every renewal is checked against the questions that decide it, with evidence from your email, meetings, CRM, support desk and usage.
  • Risk with reasons. Each account explains its score in plain English, and risk movements show what got worse since yesterday.
  • Drafts with approval. Nuva, the built-in assistant, drafts the outreach. Nothing reaches a customer until a person approves it, and you decide which routine steps may run on their own.
  • Playbooks per company and team. Steps timed against the renewal or notice date, with auto-renewing contracts handled separately.

Frequently asked questions

Is Renewal360 cheaper than Gainsight or Totango?

Renewal360 publishes its prices: a free plan for 25 accounts and paid plans from $199 a month. Gainsight and Totango price by quote, so ask them for a figure that matches your team. For most growing teams, Renewal360 costs considerably less.

Can a small team use Gainsight?

Yes, but it usually needs someone to configure and maintain it. Small teams often get more value from a focused tool they can run themselves.

Can I move from Gainsight or Totango to Renewal360 later, or the other way?

Yes. Renewal360 starts from your CRM or a CSV export, so you can run it alongside your current platform for a renewal cycle before deciding.

Does Renewal360 replace our CRM?

No. It reads your CRM, alongside email, meetings, support tickets and usage, and acts on it. The CRM stays your system of record.
