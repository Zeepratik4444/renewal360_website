If you are looking for a Totango alternative because renewals are slipping and your team is too small to run a full customer success platform, look for a tool focused on renewals: one that connects to your CRM and inbox quickly, shows what each renewal is missing, and drafts the follow-up for you. Renewal360 is built for that. If you need structured lifecycle programmes across onboarding, adoption and expansion, Totango may still be the better fit.

Totango is a capable, modular customer success platform, and since combining with Catalyst in 2024 it covers a wide range of customer programmes. Teams usually start looking elsewhere for one of three reasons: the platform is broader than they need, setting it up and keeping it running takes more effort than they can spare, or their most urgent problem is simply renewals that are slipping through. This guide helps you decide.

Why do growing teams look for a Totango alternative?

  • The problem is narrower than the platform. When renewals are leaking, a team needs every upcoming renewal checked and followed up this quarter, not a new operating model for the whole customer lifecycle.
  • Nobody owns the system. Programme-based platforms work best with someone to design and maintain the programmes. Many growing teams have no CS operations role.
  • Insight without action. Health scores and dashboards tell you an account is at risk. Someone still has to work out why and write the email.
  • Pricing is not public. Teams that want to compare costs quickly prefer published prices.

What does the first week in Renewal360 look like?

  1. Bring in your renewals. Import a CSV or connect Salesforce or HubSpot. Your renewal book appears with dates, values and owners.
  2. Connect inboxes and calendars. Each CSM connects their own Gmail or Outlook, or an admin connects a company account. You choose how much past email to read.
  3. Set your playbook. Pick the steps your team takes before a renewal and how each one runs: a reminder, a draft for approval, or, later, on its own.
  4. Work the Action Center. Each morning, see customers waiting for a reply, one-click decisions, and the calls and tasks that matter most.
  5. Approve the first drafts. Edit, approve or reject. Nothing reaches a customer until someone says so.

When is Totango still the right choice?

Stay with Totango, or choose it, if you need programmes across onboarding, adoption and expansion managed in one place, if your organisation already has people who design and run those programmes, or if you need the wider capabilities of the combined Totango and Catalyst product line. Renewal360 does not try to replace a full lifecycle platform.
